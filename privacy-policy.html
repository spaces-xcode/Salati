# Privacy Policy – Bradford Prayer Times **Last updated: November 2, 2026** ## 1. Introduction Bradford Prayer Times ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it. ## 2. Information Collection The App provides Islamic prayer timetables for Bradford, United Kingdom. The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal identification information. ## 3. Location Information The App is designed to display prayer times for Bradford, UK. It does not need access to your device's GPS location unless a future feature explicitly requests it. ## 4. Notifications and Audio The App may request permission to send browser notifications and play Adhan audio. These features are subject to your device and browser settings. ## 5. Data Storage Prayer timetable preferences and settings may be stored locally on your device. The App does not intentionally transmit this information to its developer. ## 6. Third-Party Services The App is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and browser information, in accordance with its own privacy policy. ## 7. Children's Privacy The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. ## 9.
